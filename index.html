Common Questions

What style of yoga is best for beginners?

Hatha Yoga is often recommended for beginners. It focuses on basic postures, breath control, and gentle stretching, making it accessible and safe for those new to the practice.

How often should I practice yoga to see results?

For noticeable results, aim for 3–4 sessions per week. Even 15–20 minutes of daily practice can improve flexibility, reduce stress, and build strength over time. Consistency matters more than duration.

What should I bring to my first yoga class?

Bring a yoga mat, comfortable clothing that allows movement, a water bottle, and an open mind. Most studios provide mats and props if you don't have your own.

Can yoga help with stress and anxiety?

Yes! Yoga combines breathwork, movement, and mindfulness, which activates the parasympathetic nervous system. Regular practice has been shown to lower cortisol levels and reduce symptoms of anxiety and depression.
